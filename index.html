Understanding the Difference

Key Difference: Prediction intervals are ALWAYS wider than confidence intervals because they account for both:
  1. Uncertainty in estimating the mean (same as confidence interval)
  2. Additional uncertainty about individual observations

Confidence Interval Formula:

CI: ŷ ± tα/2 × s × √(1/n + (x - x̄)²/SSx)

Shows where the TRUE MEAN RESPONSE is likely to be for a given x-value.

Prediction Interval Formula:

PI: ŷ ± tα/2 × s × √(1 + 1/n + (x - x̄)²/SSx)

Shows where an INDIVIDUAL OBSERVATION is likely to be for a given x-value.

Notice the extra "1" inside the square root for prediction intervals!
